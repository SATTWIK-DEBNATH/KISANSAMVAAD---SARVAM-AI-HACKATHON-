# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js / React, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, and Python FastAPI backend

## Users
Primary users: Indian farmers, rural agricultural workers, and extension workers (Krishi Vigyan Kendra / Kisan Call Center staff) seeking real-time, spoken agricultural advisories, crop disease guidance, market (mandi) commodity rates, and government scheme navigation in their mother tongues. Secondary users: Hackathon judges, agronomists, and rural development stakeholders.

## Product Purpose
KisanSamvaad (किसान संवाद) is a sovereign, voice-first agricultural intelligence platform and document concierge powered by Sarvam AI. It breaks the literacy and language divide in rural India through natural voice conversations across 10+ Indic languages, handles code-mixed speech (e.g. Hinglish, Tanglish), extracts structured data from regional Soil Health Cards using Sarvam Document Vision, and provides grounded, safe farming advice.

## Positioning
Unlike generic Western LLM chatbots that require typing in English and output text walls, KisanSamvaad delivers sub-second voice-in/voice-out advisories in authentic Indian accents, supports feature phone telephony paradigms, and enforces strict deterministic safety guardrails on agrochemical dosages.

## Operating Context
Used outdoors in fields, village kiosks, mandis, and homes on low-cost smartphones and feature phones. Environment includes high acoustic noise (tractors, livestock, wind) and variable network conditions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multilingual Voice-In, Voice-Out Conversational Loop (Sarvam Saaras STT + Bulbul TTS + Sarvam-1 / Indic LLM)
- Regional Document Intelligence (Sarvam Document Parse for Soil Health Cards & Khasra land records)
- Real-time Mandi Price Tracker & Weather Advisory Tool Calling
- Deterministic Agrochemical & Fertilizer Dosage Guardrails (zero-tolerance for pesticide hallucination)
- Live API & Interactive Simulation Mode (resilient demo execution with or without active Sarvam cloud credits)

## Brand Commitments
- Name: KisanSamvaad (किसान संवाद) - "Empowering Every Indian Farmer with Sovereign Voice AI"
- Voice Persona: Respectful, clear, empathetic, practical rural agricultural expert (Krishi Mitra)

## Evidence on Hand
- Sarvam AI APIs (Saaras v3/v4 ASR, Bulbul v3/v4 TTS, Mayura translation, Sarvam-1, Document Parse)
- ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) agronomic standards & Soil Health Card NPK metrics
- Challenge Track: Track 03 (Agriculture and Rural Innovation)

## Product Principles
1. Voice First & Literacy Free: Every essential workflow can be completed entirely through speech and audio.
2. Grounded Truth & Safety First: Chemical treatments and fertilizer recommendations are strictly validated against verified agricultural databases.
3. Authentic Sovereign Voice: Native Indian prosody, regional number/currency pronunciation (Quintal, Bigha, ₹), and code-mixed resilience.
4. Resilient & Accessible: Instant visual feedback, outdoor high-contrast theme, and dual live/demo operation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Designed for high legibility under direct sunlight, prominent audio play buttons, large touch targets, and support for Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Punjabi, Gujarati, Odia, and English.
