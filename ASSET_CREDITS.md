# KisanSamvaad Asset Credits & Art Direction Protocol

## 1. Imagery Ethics & Policy

In strict accordance with Section 6 of the Master Redesign Brief:
- **No Fabricated Farmers**: KisanSamvaad strictly prohibits using AI-generated portraits of people presented as real Indian farmers.
- **Dignity & Authenticity**: All imagery represents authentic agricultural landscapes, real crops, verified Soil Health Cards, and actual mandi trading yards.
- **Open Licensing & Attribution**: Only public domain, CC0, or openly licensed assets are used, with full attribution recorded below.

## 3. Art Direction Brief for Future Photography Slots

When physical field photography is added to the platform, photographers and designers must adhere to these specifications:

### Slot 1: Hero Agricultural Landscape (`assets/hero_kolar_fields.webp`)
- **Subject**: Tomato and vegetable trellis fields in Kolar / Chikkaballapur at dawn or late afternoon.
- **Lighting**: Natural golden hour sunlight showing red sandy loam soil and lush green vine foliage.
- **Composition**: Wide cinematic ratio (16:9 desktop, 4:5 mobile crop), subject placed along lower third, natural sky gradient in upper half.
- **Prohibited**: Heavy HDR saturation, studio lighting, corporate actors posing unnaturally.

### Slot 2: Physical Soil Health Card Scanning (`assets/soil_card_field_scan.webp`)
- **Subject**: A worn, creased physical Soil Health Card resting on a rustic wooden table or field bench in Mandya.
- **Lighting**: Diffuse natural daylight from a side window or veranda shade.
- **Composition**: 4:3 ratio, overhead 45-degree angle showing handwritten laboratory test figures.

### Slot 3: APMC Mandi Trading Yard (`assets/mandi_kolar_market.webp`)
- **Subject**: Authentic auction yard crates of tomatoes and onions at Kolar APMC trading platform.
- **Lighting**: Bright early morning market activity.
- **Composition**: Documentary-style depth of field showing produce crates in foreground with trading boards in background.
