02 / THE AGRICULTURAL REALITY
CHALLENGES FACED BY 140M+ FARMERS

THE PEOPLE WHO FEED THE NATION DESERVE BETTER ACCESS TO INTELLIGENCE.

Agricultural universities and ICAR institutions generate breakthrough agronomic science every season. Yet smallholder farmers remain excluded because actionable intelligence is trapped behind complex English portals, tabular Soil Health Cards, and volatile market middlemen.

Field Reality in Kolar & Mandya:

Farmers spend an average of ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 extra per acre applying excessive Urea and DAP simply because soil test numbers are difficult to interpret without expert guidance.

01 / CRITICAL BARRIER LANGUAGE MISMATCH

Regional Dialects vs. English Prompts

Over 84% of smallholder farmers speak regional dialects (Kannada, Hindi, Telugu, Marathi). Generic English-centric LLMs fail on code-mixed terms like "Kapas me thrips lag gaya" or "Tomato ele suruli roga".

02 / HARDWARE EXCLUSION 2G FEATURE PHONES

Smartphone Assumption Fallacy

Most digital agri-apps assume high-end touchscreens and 4G connectivity. Rural India still relies heavily on ₹1,000 keypad phones where interactive voice telephony (IVR) is the only truly universal medium.

03 / DOCUMENT OBTUSENESS SOIL HEALTH CARDS

Paper Reports With No Translation

Over 230 million physical Soil Health Cards have been distributed in India. Yet farmers cannot convert abstract laboratory readings (e.g. "P: 8.4 kg/ha, pH: 5.8") into bags of compost or fertilizer dosages.

04 / MARKET ASYMMETRY APMC ARBITRAGE

Delayed Mandi Arrival Quotes

Price disparities between neighboring markets (e.g. Kolar vs Yeshwanthpur) often exceed ₹400 per quintal. Without real-time voice quotes, farmers sell at distress rates at their farm gate.

05 / SAFETY HAZARDS CHEMICAL HALLUCINATIONS

Unregulated AI Advice Risks Crop Ruin

Standard commercial chatbots frequently hallucinate toxic pesticide dilutions or recommend banned substances (Endosulfan, Paraquat). Agriculture demands deterministic, fail-closed ICAR guardrails.

08 / RESPONSIBLE AGRICULTURAL AI
DETERMINISTIC ICAR SAFETY BOUNDS

INTELLIGENCE MUST BE RESPONSIBLE.

Stochastic language models cannot be trusted with lethal agricultural chemicals. KisanSamvaad isolates chemical and fertilizer recommendations behind a deterministic, rule-based ICAR guardrail engine that fails closed on prohibited substances.

🚫

CIBRC Banned Pesticide Interceptor

Strictly intercepts Endosulfan, Monocrotophos, Paraquat, Methyl Parathion, and DDT. Queries referencing these trigger an automatic emergency safety warning to consult a local KVK officer.

⚖️

Overdose Boundary Clamping

Hard caps on chemical dilutions (e.g. Imidacloprid ≤ 0.5 ml/L, Chlorpyrifos ≤ 2.5 ml/L, Foliar Urea ≤ 2%). Hallucinated 10x concentrations are intercepted before reaching the farmer.

🩺

Human Expert Escalation

Uncertain diagnoses automatically direct farmers to their nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or agricultural extension officer with exact contact coordinates.
